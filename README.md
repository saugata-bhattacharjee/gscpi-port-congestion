# Supply Chain Pressure and Consumer Prices

How long does it take for supply chain problems to show up on supermarket shelves?

I used the **Global Supply Chain Pressure Index (GSCPI)** from the New York Fed and compared it with consumer price data to see how pressure in shipping and ports moves into inflation. I wrote the findings up as a LinkedIn article.

## Main points

- Supply chain pressure shows up in prices with a delay, not at once.
- Small bullwhip effects inside supply chains add up to cost pressure at a macro level.
- Prices go up faster than they come down ("sticky prices").
